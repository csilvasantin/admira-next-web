Where NVIDIA fits

Three jobs, three kinds of compute.

1Inference at the edge. The room decides locally — audience, context and content — under 200 ms, and no camera feed ever leaves the building. Today on ThinkEdge with an NVIDIA GPU per space.
2The twin, reconstructed. A phone walk-through becomes a 3D twin. Reconstruction is GPU work, not CPU work: it belongs next to the space, on a compact NVIDIA node.
3Models that stay in the store. We already run local inference on a GB10 node in our own fleet — the same shape a retailer needs when the data cannot go to a cloud region.
